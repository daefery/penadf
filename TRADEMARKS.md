The source code is open under the [Apache License 2.0](LICENSE). The **PenaDF** name, logo (the page with a pen nib) and app icon are not: section 6 of that licence grants no rights to trade names or marks.

You may:

- fork, modify and ship the code, under Apache-2.0;
- say that your project is "based on PenaDF" or "a fork of PenaDF".

Please don't:

- publish a fork, build or product under the PenaDF name or with its logo or icon;
- imply that a modified version is the official PenaDF.

If you ship a fork, give it its own name and replace `assets/penadf.*`, `installer/wizard.bmp`, the names in `installer/penadf.nsi` and `build.rs`, and the ProgID in `src/winshell.rs`.
