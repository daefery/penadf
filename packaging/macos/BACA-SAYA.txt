PenaDF for Mac

1. Drag PenaDF onto the Applications folder.
2. Open PenaDF from Applications. macOS will say it cannot check the app for
   malicious software, because PenaDF is not notarized by Apple yet.
3. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click
   "Open Anyway" next to PenaDF. Then open PenaDF again and click "Open".
   You only do this once.

To open PDFs with PenaDF when you double-click them: in Finder, select any PDF,
press Command-I (Get Info), choose PenaDF under "Open with", then click
"Change All...".

PenaDF works offline. Your files never leave your Mac.
Source code and help: https://github.com/daefery/penadf

PenaDF untuk Mac

1. Seret PenaDF ke folder Applications.
2. Buka PenaDF dari Applications. macOS akan bilang aplikasi ini tidak bisa
   diperiksa, karena PenaDF belum dinotarisasi oleh Apple.
3. Buka System Settings > Privacy & Security, gulir ke bawah, lalu klik
   "Open Anyway" di samping PenaDF. Buka PenaDF lagi dan klik "Open".
   Cukup sekali saja.

Agar PDF langsung terbuka di PenaDF saat diklik dua kali: di Finder, pilih
salah satu PDF, tekan Command-I (Get Info), pilih PenaDF di bagian
"Open with", lalu klik "Change All...".

PenaDF bekerja tanpa internet. File Anda tidak pernah keluar dari Mac Anda.
